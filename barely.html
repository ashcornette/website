Overview

Each app started with the same question: what if we removed everything except the one thing that matters?

Notes apps compete on folders. Spending trackers compete on categories. Journals compete on prompts. Every app in every category assumes more structure means more clarity.

The constraint isn't a limitation. It's the product.

No app in the suite will get a second core feature. If a new idea needs a new surface, it becomes a new app.

Design Language

All three share Bokashi themes (Japanese colour gradients), time-aware colour shifting, the same settings structure, and the same accessibility standards. They look like siblings, not clones.

What I Learned

Building three apps solo in five months taught me that scope is a design decision. The hardest part wasn't building. It was saying no to good ideas that would make the apps worse.

Drawing tools in Notes. Categories in Spent. Streaks in Said. All tested well. All broke the product.
