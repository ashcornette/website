About

For twenty years I was breakinghip-hop style of street dance. You improvise live, in front of people, with no second takes, and you learn to cut everything down to the one move that lands.

A brief is the same problem. Too many good ideas fighting for the same small space, and the whole job is choosing what to cut.

Brands, products, the web. Strategy through launch.

Currently taking on new projects. Discovery call to proposal in one week.

"We had years of content and no clear direction. Ash gathered what mattered, cut the rest, and it connected."
